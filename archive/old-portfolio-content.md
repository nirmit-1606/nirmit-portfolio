## TutorReserve

**URL:** https://nirmitpatel.online/tutorreserve/

### Overview

TutorReserve is a booking application designed to help parents and guardians find reliable tutors based on their child's specific needs. Typical users range from 25 to 60 years old, consisting of busy parents balancing work and home life, as well as guardians caring for children.

**Project Duration:** 4 weeks (August 2024 – September 2024)

### The Problem

There is a lack of easy access to trustworthy local tutors, and finding a schedule that works for both parents and tutors can be a challenge.

### The Goal

Design a mobile application that allows users to find reliable tutors with flexible schedules. The booking process should be intuitive and simple to navigate.

### My Role

Lead UX designer and researcher

### Responsibilities

User research, conducting interviews, paper and digital wireframing, low- and high-fidelity prototyping, conducting usability studies, ensuring accessibility, iterating on designs, and creating responsive designs.

---

### User Research

**Summary**

Conducted secondary research due to time constraints and limited access to potential interviewees. Data gathered from online sources like forums, reviews of existing tutoring platforms, and tools like ChatGPT. Initial assumptions about priorities were challenged — safety, trustworthiness, and flexible scheduling were equally significant for both groups.

**Pain Points**

**Finding Qualified Tutors**
Parents struggle to find tutors who match their child's learning style and academic needs. A robust search and filtering system would allow users to filter tutors by qualifications, subjects, and teaching styles.

**Safety and Reliability Concerns**
Parents are concerned about safety and tutor credibility. Incorporating verified profiles, background checks, and reviews can build trust between parents and tutors.

**Inconsistent Tutor Availability**
Tutors face challenges with inconsistent bookings. A flexible scheduling system allows tutors to manage their availability while promoting their services within the app.

**Accessibility and Payment**
Both parents and tutors experience difficulties with scheduling and payment. A secure and integrated payment system would streamline these processes.

---

### Persona: Carlos Rodriguez

**Problem Statement**
Carlos is a father who needs to find a reliable tutor and book sessions for his children, but faces limited availability of qualified tutors.

**User Journey Map**
Mapping out Carlos's journey highlighted the need for a user-friendly interface that simplifies the process of finding and booking tutors, as well as the importance of a secure payment system to build trust.

---

### Design Process

**User Flow Diagram**
A user flow visually maps out the steps a user takes to interact with TutorReserve. It helps illustrate key actions, decision points, and the screens users will encounter.

**Storyboards**

- Big-Picture Storyboard: Focuses on the users' broader needs, showing how TutorReserve helps parents like Carlos in real-world scenarios, ensuring convenience and trust.
- Close-Up Storyboard: Offers a detailed view of the app's interface, highlighting key interactions such as tutor browsing, booking, and payments.

**Information Architecture (IA)**
The primary pain point was difficulty navigating tutor profiles and booking pages. An IA was created to strategically improve navigation, making it straightforward and intuitive.

**Paper Wireframes**
Initial paper wireframes were sketched with a focus on simplifying the navigation, improving the booking process, and making the search/filtering system accessible.

**Digital Wireframes**
Transitioning from paper to digital wireframes provided clarity on how design adjustments could address user pain points. Priority was given to clear visual hierarchy, easy button placement, and improved layout for better user flow.

**Low-Fidelity Prototype**
Connected screens to create a low-fidelity prototype demonstrating the primary user flow: browsing for a tutor, selecting a date and time to book the session, and payment. Feedback from peers and colleagues highlighted button placement and organization improvements.

---

### Usability Testing

**Findings**

- **Session:** Users requested an option to modify the booked session.
- **Scheduling:** Users wanted the ability to schedule recurring sessions more easily.
- **Navigation:** Some users were confused by the layout during the booking process.

**Updating Wireframes**

*Booking Process:* Combined the day and time selection into a single page to simplify the slot selection process. This reduced confusion and improved the user experience.

*Recurring Sessions:* A checkbox for recurring bookings was added, allowing users to easily schedule multiple sessions without repeating the process.

---

### High-Fidelity Design

The wireframe layout was converted to a high-fidelity mockup with rich typography, colors, iconography, and elevation, along with a responsive layout.

Saved reusable elements as components, making future updates easier. Using instances of components allowed for consistent design and quick changes across all screens.

The high-fidelity prototype reflected the findings from usability testing, integrating all design improvements, including simplified navigation and improved accessibility features.

---

### Accessibility Considerations

- **Clear Visual Hierarchy:** Appropriately sized headings used to establish clear visual hierarchy for better readability.
- **Landmarks for Navigation:** Incorporating landmarks made the app more accessible for users relying on assistive technologies.
- **Color Contrast:** Strong color contrast ensured that users with visual impairments could navigate the app with ease.

---

### Takeaways

**Impact**
Users appreciated the intuitive booking process and the ability to find local tutors through the app. The streamlined scheduling system and easy navigation significantly improved their experience.

**What I Learned**
Even small design changes can have a large impact on user satisfaction. By focusing on the real needs of users, design solutions were created that cater to both parents' concerns about safety and tutors' need for flexible scheduling.

**Next Steps**
- Conduct further usability testing on the updated mockups.
- Incorporate additional options, such as specifying days for recurring bookings.
- Create a web version of the app to provide easy access without downloading.

## Camping Supplies Store

**URL:** https://nirmitpatel.online/camping-supplies-store/

### Research & Insights

Conducted interviews with five users to gather requirements and insights. Created personas including Sarah (a beginner camper), Mark (a busy professional), Dave (an experienced camper), and Emily (a casual camper). Defined key problems, hypotheses, and goal statements to address unique needs, from beginner guides to rental options.

**Deliverables:**
- 4 different types of personas
- Journey maps of 4 personas
- Problem statements, hypothesis statements, and goal statements for each persona

### Competitive Audit

Reviewed competitors including KingCamp, REI, Cabela's, Walmart, and Amazon. Identified gaps like beginner-friendly resources and streamlined navigation. Defined opportunities to build a user-friendly site tailored to beginners, showcase clear product features, and offer engaging, accessible guides.

### Design & Development

Developed wireframes and high-fidelity mockups with iterative refinements, including color and icon changes for better accessibility and usability. Used Pikaso for AI-generated realistic images, finding it effective for style selection and batch image options. Implemented a design system to ensure consistency across all elements.

### Takeaways

- **Enhanced Figma Skills:** Improved proficiency in Figma through complex wireframing and high-fidelity mockup creation.
- **Utilized AI Tools:** Learned to effectively use AI tools like Pikaso for generating custom images, improving visual consistency in prototyping.
- **User-Centered Design Focus:** Gained a deeper understanding of user needs, resulting in more effective and intuitive design solutions.
- **Valuable Usability Testing:** Conducted usability tests that indicated strong user satisfaction, confirming that the design was easy to navigate and find key elements.
- **Accessibility Improvements:** Made informed design choices to enhance color accessibility and overall user experience.

### Next Steps

- Complete remaining pages to develop a full user journey prototype.
- Gather more user feedback for continuous improvement.
- Implement iterative refinements based on ongoing testing and user insights.
- Make the design compatible with different device sizes (mobile and tablet).
